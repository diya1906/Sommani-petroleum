About the outlet

An IndianOil pump built for the South Bopal commute.

Sommani Petroleum sits right on the Ambli–Bopal stretch of SP Ring Road, at the point most locals already use as a landmark — the corner by Vakil Saheb Bridge and the KIA Motors showroom. It's a straightforward stop for petrol and diesel, with an air bay for a quick nitrogen top-up on the way through.

Forecourt staff run the pumps day and night, so whether it's an early commute toward SG Highway or a late return from South Bopal, there's fuel and air without a detour.
